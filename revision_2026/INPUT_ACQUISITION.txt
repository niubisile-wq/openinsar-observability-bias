External inputs for the current scientific revision

The reviewer checkout bundles attributed processed analysis inputs, Census subsets, two Fresno population grids, nine cropped raw DWR displacement exports, and the fixed-ROI DWR observation-cell polygons. Selected complete provider files are also supplied in the versioned companion assets listed below; complete original interferogram stacks are separate. Preserve source-provider terms for both bundled and newly acquired materials.
The input manifests give the retained hashes; a live provider may update byte serialization or product versions.
Do not substitute a changed input silently. Compare identities, geometries, vintages and reported checksums.

3. Fresno observation footprint and fine population grid
https://gis.water.ca.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Elevation/Vertical_Displacement_Point_Data_Locations_2026Q1/MapServer/0
Query fixed ROI [-119.9,36.6,-119.7,36.85] in WGS84. core/fetch_dwr.py preserves the query and retrieves
38,738 actual observation-cell polygons, plus the fine GHSL tile. core/experiment_dwr.py builds the retained
results/dwr/grid.npz. The layer represents observation footprints, not invented point buffers. The retained query is bundled at
data/strengthening/external/dwr/roi_features.json; the processed integration grid is at
data/strengthening/results/dwr/grid.npz. The provider route is for reacquisition, not a prerequisite
for inspecting the bundled polygons. Preserve California DWR/TRE ALTAMIRA attribution and provider conditions.

4. Fresno Census blocks
https://services.arcgis.com/P3ePLMYs2RVChkJx/ArcGIS/rest/services/USA_Census_2020_DHC_Blocks/FeatureServer/1
Service item 87cc27d8181a46ec974301ece2a2575a. Query GEOID prefix 06019 and the same WGS84 ROI using spatial
intersection. Retrieve GEOID, P0010001, ALAND, AWATER and geometry; order by GEOID and page at 1,000 records.
The retained seven GeoJSON pages contain 6,999 unique intersecting blocks; the analysis retains 6,788 complete
blocks with 523,953 Census residents. Place pages at census_blocks/fresno_roi/blocks_*.geojson.
The service identifies TIGER-derived cartographic boundaries and 2020 DHC counts; differential privacy applies.
An official Census re-download requires a new geometry/count comparison before claiming exact equivalence.

5. Fresno DWR vertical-rate rasters
https://gis.water.ca.gov/arcgisimg/rest/services/SAR/Vertical_Displacement_TRE_ALTAMIRA_Annual_Rate_Mosaic/ImageServer
Obtain raw floating-point annual rasters, with no classification render function. Document raster catalog
selection and dates. The six annual windows are October 2015-October 2016 through October 2020-October 2021.
Additional sensitivities use October 2023-October 2024, April 2025-April 2026 and the separate cumulative
June 2015-January 2021 product. Exact filenames, source items, DateFrom/DateTo, export requests and SHA-256 hashes are in
data/workspace/dwr_velocity/raw_input_manifest.json; the current SI methods are in S28.
The nine retained exports have 279 rows x 177 columns, EPSG:4326, source-grid alignment, nearest-neighbor
export and adjustAspectRatio=false (the ImageServer size parameter is width,height = 177,279).
Raw values are interval displacement in feet, including products named Annual_Rate. Convert each window as
mm/year = raw feet * 304.8 / ((DateTo - DateFrom in days) / 365.2425). Do not treat raw values as feet/year
or multiply by 304.8 alone. The April 2025-April 2026 interval differs from the October-to-October windows;
the cumulative June 2015-January 2021 product uses its own exact interval.
The raw TIFF crops, annual/cumulative service catalogs, service metadata and manifest are bundled at
data/workspace/dwr_velocity/. Run reproduce.py dwr-rate-suite --workspace OUTPUT for the annualization
and downstream class tables. Reacquire with followup/fetch_dwr_rate_inputs.py --output OUTPUT and compare
the retained hashes; live ImageServer exports may differ. These are selected cropped provider exports,
not the complete statewide DWR archive. Preserve California DWR/TRE ALTAMIRA attribution and applicable
provider conditions; the authors do not relicense these third-party materials.
This paragraph supersedes the pre-3-October acquisition guide wording that called the raw values
feet/year and said the cropped exports and observation polygons were absent from the code archive.
