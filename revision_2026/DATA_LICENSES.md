Full source archives, original processing stacks, proprietary software, local credentials and private submission-account state are not included. Hashes and native units/vintages must be checked before replacing an input. Different provider serialization or revised live products may prevent byte-identical raw reconstruction even when an analysis remains scientifically comparable.
