Some complete source files are distributed in the v0.4.1 ACS/Census and v0.5.0 selected-source companion assets; `INPUT_ACQUISITION.txt` states their exact scope. The full original interferogram/processing stacks, CA-POP source raster, proprietary software, local credentials and private submission-account state are not included. Hashes and native units/vintages must be checked before replacing an input. Different provider serialization or revised live products may prevent byte-identical raw reconstruction even when an analysis remains scientifically comparable.

This inventory supersedes the earlier blanket statement that DWR source crops and observation polygons were not redistributed. It describes the retained files and preserves provider terms; it does not grant additional rights to third-party materials. The 2026-10-03 local audit archives carry their own input provenance and provider notices.
